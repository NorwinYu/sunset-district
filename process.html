The skill used

photoreal-gamea procedure for playable, photographic 3D from code

Everything in this build followed the photoreal-game skill: product before look; one dominant light with an environment for fill; material variation in albedo, roughness and normal; multi-scale composition; a filmic image formation chain; and executable gates instead of opinion.

The skill's order mattered. The title, race loop, settings, pause, save, touch path and failure states existed before the city was lit. Each art round changed one lever, was captured from fixed cameras, and was kept only if no gate regressed.

The subjectA wet city circuit at golden hour, with a code-built kart as the hero object.
The signature systemWet asphalt with a half-resolution planar reflection, puddles and roughness-masked Fresnel.
The verdictsProduct, mobile, census, lights, playtest and look gates — the gate wins over taste.

01 · Product

Title, race, pause, settings, save, touch, win/lose and restart are built first. A beautiful frame without a loop is a failure.

02 · Light

One shadow-casting sun down the canyon, a PMREM sky environment, a sky-tinted hemisphere and a cool bounce from the camera side.

03 · Materials

Procedural asphalt, paving, four facade styles, metal, concrete, wood, rubber, cloth and water — all with more than one varied channel.

04 · Composition

Kart, barrier, sidewalk, street wall, landmark towers and a distant skyline. Three scales and three depth layers in the hero frame.

05 · Image

HDR scene target, ACES tone mapping, bloom, FXAA, chromatic aberration, grain, vignette and a deliberate desaturation.

06 · Gates

Render, census, collision, lights, product, mobile, playtest and look. The stock walker gates were re-expressed for a barrier-lined circuit.

The record

Six rounds

Every round was captured from a fixed camera and a fixed seed. The next change came from the lowest gate/metric, not from the most interesting idea.

00

First light

The whole pipeline existed before the art pass: an inlined Three.js, a seeded city, a spline circuit, procedural karts, a sky and a post chain. The first frame exposed gross defects: barriers rotated across the track, the hero camera inside the grandstand roof, a neon palette and a title card covering the cars.
01

Correct the circuit, compose the grid

The player moved to the back-right grid slot and the camera inside the barrier line. The grandstand moved off the sightline. The barriers became continuous concrete-and-steel walls. A dimensioned city fabric replaced the empty ground. Top-down check: the loop, barriers and city blocks read as a circuit rather than a prop ring.
02

Materials and colour

The 5 × 5 facade atlas gained recessed windows, sills, blinds and normal maps. The asphalt gained gutter/rut wetness and a wet roughness map. A composite desaturation brought measured colourfulness from 107.6 → 40.6 and midtones from 51.8% → 59.6%. Spectators, awnings and shop signs filled the street.
colourfulness 40.6midtones 59.6%clip 0.69/0.72
03

The signature surface

A half-resolution mirrored camera renders the city except the road. The wet asphalt samples it through a Fresnel × roughness mask, a ripple warp and a four-tap blur. This one system carried the “wet sunset street” idea into every race frame; it is the visual leap of the whole project.
planar reflectionHIGH / ULTRA only
04

Hero object, city density

The tyre cylinder was hiding the rims: the sidewalls were solid caps. Opening them exposed the rims, spokes, brake discs and lettering. A wedge nose, rear wing, number plates, harness and engine detail made the kart nameable at 2.6 m. The background gained a dense block grid, setbacks, rooftop units and two landmark towers.
05

Product shell and a real loop

Title, settings, pause, save, touch, win, loss and restart; four laps with five AI rivals, drift/nitro, boost pads, cone hazards, a 4:00 clock and a health bar. The product gate caught a real defect: the pause/settings modal computed to opacity 0 because of a fade-in animation. The screens must be visible in the DOM, not only in a screenshot.

06

Tone, evidence, final captures

The final pair of stills crushed blacks to 7.24% (hero) and 12.1% (close). A shadow lift, a softer vignette, more sky bounce and a tighter lit close camera moved the hero to 1.63% and the close to 2.08%; flat tiles on the close fell from 15% to 7%. Both cameras passed the fallback tone + emptiness gate.

What is still not photoreal

  • The kart is a code-authored low-poly kit. At 2.6 m the wing, seat and arms still read as designed primitives, not a manufactured vehicle.
  • The city is box massing with a facade atlas. From above it reads as a block diagram; from the street the facades carry the scene.
  • The spectator set is intentionally minimal; the crowd is two instanced draws, not individually modelled people.
  • No external reference imagery was permitted, so the look gate is tone + emptiness, not a similarity score.
  • No mobile GPU profile was captured; the mobile preset defaults to MEDIUM and the quality selector exposes LOW–ULTRA.

What the player can do

  • Race four laps from P6 to the podium; drift for nitro, release for mini-turbo, spend it on the straights.
  • Hit six boost pads, dodge three cone zones, survive barriers and rival contact; repair at each lap.
  • Win at P1–P3, lose to the 4:00 clock, a wrecked chassis, or a P4+ finish; restart in one action.
  • Persist best lap and best finish; change sensitivity, volume, FOV, invert-Y, quality and mute.
  • Play with keyboard + mouse or touch at 390 × 844; look around with a drag.

Privacy & data

No analytics, cookies, tracking, external requests or personal data. The game stores only the best lap, best position and settings in localStorage. The screenshots were captured headlessly at a fixed seed; the names in the game are fictional. The site is static: no server, no account, no comments.
